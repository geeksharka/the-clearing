What is The Clearing

A place where the noise stops and the thinking begins.

The Clearing is a network of world-class natural retreat destinations — set on coastlines, in high desert light, and deep in mountain forests — where professional teams and groups come to do their clearest thinking and most purposeful work.

Each location offers exceptional lodging, communal dining crafted from quality ingredients, Body & Soul amenities, guided Experiences that connect teams to the land and its people, and purpose-built programming for the groups who need more than a change of scenery.

"The best conversations happen somewhere worth having them."

The Airstream Fleet

An icon in every landscape.

Each Clearing location maintains a fleet of pre-installed Airstreams — styled, appointed, and positioned by The Clearing team. No towing. No setup. Just arrival.

Nestled into a Santa Fe mesa at dusk, perched above the Big Sur coastline, tucked into the Blue Ridge hills — the Airstream is The Clearing's signature accommodation. Nostalgic, genuine, and impossible not to photograph.

The Table

Grounded cooking.
World-class meals.

Every Clearing location has a full café and restaurant on site. The food is honest — quality ingredients, prepared with care, served at long communal tables where the conversations that matter tend to happen.

This isn't conference catering. It's the kind of meal that becomes part of the story your team tells when they get home. Shared plates, unhurried evenings, and the kind of nourishment that makes the next day's work feel possible.

Local sourcing. Seasonal menus. The same warmth in a bowl of morning porridge as in a long evening dinner under the open sky.

Why The Clearing Exists

"The most important conversations about technology happen best somewhere it hasn't arrived yet."

We live in a world of permanent connection — notifications, feeds, inboxes, pings. The quality of our thinking, our relationships, and our decisions suffers quietly for it. The Clearing exists on a different premise: that the natural world, shared meals, and the presence of other people in the same room are not retreats from productive work. They are the conditions that make breakthrough work possible.

About
The Clearing

The Clearing began with a simple observation: the most important problems facing organizations today — how to build responsibly with data, how to scale technology without losing what's human, how to make good decisions in a world of infinite noise — are rarely solved in the environments where they're supposed to be solved.

We built The Clearing as an answer to that. A network of destinations in places of world-class natural beauty — the high desert of New Mexico, the dramatic Pacific coastline of Big Sur, the ancient forests of the Blue Ridge — where the setting itself does some of the work. Where removal from the daily environment isn't an indulgence but a precondition for clear thinking.

Each location serves professional teams and groups seeking a different kind of productivity. Our Airstream fleet, private cabins, and group lodges accommodate every configuration. Our on-site restaurant brings everyone to the same communal table. Our Body & Soul amenities — yoga studio, gym, and Zen gardens — ground the body so the mind can do its best work. Our guided Experiences connect teams to each landscape and its people.

And for the teams who need more than a beautiful setting — The Forge and The Spark offer something no other retreat venue provides: a permanent, resident team of recognized experts in data, technology, scaling, and security, ready to work alongside your team on the problems that matter most.

The Clearing is built on the conviction that technology and human life are not in opposition — but that understanding how to hold them together requires stepping back from both. That's what we're here for.
