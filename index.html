What is The Clearing

A place where the noise stops and the thinking begins.

The Clearing is a network of world-class natural retreat destinations — set on coastlines, in mountain meadows, and deep in forested valleys — where professional teams, curious individuals, and small groups come to do their best work and their most human living.

Each location offers exceptional lodging, family-style dining crafted from quality ingredients, guided Experiences that connect you to the land and its people, and purpose-built programming for the teams who need more than a change of scenery.

Why The Clearing Exists

"The most important conversations about technology happen best somewhere it hasn't arrived yet."

We live in a world of permanent connection — notifications, feeds, inboxes, pings. The quality of our thinking, our relationships, and our decisions suffers quietly for it. The Clearing exists on a different premise: that the natural world, shared meals, and the presence of other human beings are not retreats from productive life. They are the conditions that make it possible.

About
The Clearing

The Clearing began with a simple observation: the most important problems facing organizations today — how to build responsibly with data, how to scale technology without losing what's human, how to make good decisions in a world of infinite noise — are rarely solved in the environments where they're supposed to be solved.

We built The Clearing as an answer to that. A network of destinations in places of world-class natural beauty — the high desert of New Mexico, the wild Pacific coast of British Columbia, the ancient forests of the Blue Ridge — where the setting itself does some of the work. Where removal from the daily environment isn't an indulgence but a precondition for clear thinking.

Each location is designed to hold two kinds of guests without compromise: the individual or family seeking genuine rest and natural immersion, and the professional team seeking a different kind of productivity. Our Airstream fleet, private cabins, and group lodges serve both. Our family-style restaurant brings everyone to the same table. Our guided Experiences connect guests to each landscape and its people.

And for the teams who need more than a beautiful setting — The Forge offers something no other retreat venue provides: a permanent, resident team of recognized experts in data, technology, scaling, and security, ready to work alongside your team on the problems that matter most.

The Clearing is built on the conviction that technology and human life are not in opposition — but that understanding how to hold them together requires stepping back from both. That's what we're here for.
